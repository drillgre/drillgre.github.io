Frequently Asked Questions

A quick guide to using DrillGRE.

What is DrillGRE?

DrillGRE is a GRE preparation platform offering Quant lessons, question practice, quizzes, mock tests and vocabulary learning tools.

How do I access Quant lessons?

Open Quant Academy, choose a module and select a published lesson. Lesson content and linked practice questions are loaded from the course catalogue.

Do I need an account?

You can explore available public learning content. An account is required for features that save learner-specific progress or use authenticated dashboard services.

Are lessons and practice questions linked?

Published lessons may include associated practice questions. When questions are linked, you can submit an answer and view the result and available explanation.

Does DrillGRE save my progress?

DrillGRE uses browser storage and, for supported signed-in features, Supabase to save learning activity and progress. Availability may vary by feature and account status.

How do I use the dashboard?

Sign in and open your learner dashboard to review available study activity, performance analytics, recommendations and study-plan features. Some analytics depend on recorded attempts and completed setup.

How are mock-test scores used?

Mock-test attempts can contribute to score progression and learner performance summaries when the relevant attempt data is recorded.

Can I reset my progress?

Progress may be stored locally and in your account depending on the feature. Contact info@drillgre.com for help identifying the appropriate reset process.

Is DrillGRE affiliated with ETS?

DrillGRE is an independent GRE preparation resource and is not affiliated with or endorsed by ETS. GRE is a registered trademark of Educational Testing Service.

How can I report a problem or send feedback?

Email info@drillgre.com with the page URL and a description of the issue.
