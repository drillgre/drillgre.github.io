Scoring

Correct answers earn points according to difficulty: Level 1 = 1 point, Level 2 = 2, Level 3 = 3, Level 4 = 4, and Level 5 = 5. Incorrect and unanswered questions receive zero points. There is no negative marking.

Scaled score: 130 + 40 × (weighted points earned ÷ weighted points available). This is an initial DrillGRE scale, not an official ETS score conversion.

There is no timer in this first version. Your in-progress answers are saved in this browser, and you can resume after refreshing the page.
