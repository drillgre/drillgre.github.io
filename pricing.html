Pricing questions

Is DrillGRE really free?

Yes. DrillGRE does not currently charge for access to its learning platform and does not offer a paid subscription tier.

Do I need a credit card?

No. You do not need to enter payment details to create an account or use the platform.

Do I need an account?

You can explore public learning content without signing in. Create a free account to use features that save learner-specific activity and progress.

Will DrillGRE introduce paid plans?

DrillGRE is currently offered free of charge. If access arrangements change in the future, the details will be communicated clearly on this page.
