How to win

  • A word challenge appears and the 20-second clock starts.
  • Choose one of the four options. Your selection triggers the opponent's arrow.
  • Fire your answer. A correct shot meets the opponent's arrow in a bright collision burst, then the next word appears automatically.
  • A wrong answer, or a timeout, lets the opponent's arrow hit your shield. You lose one life.
  • Earn 10 points per bullseye and 5 bonus points for every third consecutive hit.
