Create one shared chart, table, graph, or data context, then link exactly three Data Analysis questions to it. The mock test presents the stimulus once and keeps the three questions together.

After saving, select this group for each of its three questions. For mock eligibility, the three published questions must be Level 2, 3, and 4 for Section 1; Level 1, 2, and 3 for the easier Section 2 route; or Level 3, 4, and 5 for the harder Section 2 route.
