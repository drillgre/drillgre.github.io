Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DrillGRE handles information when you use the website, create an account, study lessons, practise questions or use learner features.

1. Information we may collect

Depending on how you use DrillGRE, information may include:

  • Account information, such as your email address and authentication identifiers.
  • Learning activity, including lesson progress, quiz and mock-test attempts, answers, scores and study-plan entries.
  • Technical information required to operate the website, maintain security and diagnose errors, such as browser or device information and request logs handled by service providers.
  • Information you send to us when you contact support.

2. How information is used

Information is used to provide account access, deliver lessons and practice, save learning progress, generate performance insights, operate platform features, maintain security, troubleshoot problems and respond to enquiries.

3. Storage and service providers

DrillGRE uses Supabase for database and authentication services. Information needed to provide the platform may be processed and stored by Supabase under its applicable terms and privacy practices. The website is hosted through GitHub Pages. These providers may process technical information as part of delivering their services.

4. Cookies and local storage

DrillGRE may use browser storage, including local storage, to remember preferences, tour status and learning progress or to support platform functionality. Authentication and other integrated services may also use cookies or similar technologies where required.

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that help operate the website, or disclosed where required by law, to protect the rights and security of users or the platform, or with your direction.

6. Retention and security

Information is retained for as long as reasonably needed to provide the service, maintain records and meet legitimate operational or legal requirements. Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your choices and requests

You may stop using the service and contact us to request access, correction or deletion of personal information associated with your account. Some information may need to be retained where required for security, legal or operational reasons. You can also manage browser storage through your browser settings; clearing it may affect saved local preferences or progress.

8. Children

DrillGRE is designed as a GRE preparation service and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the request.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the platform changes. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact info@drillgre.com.
